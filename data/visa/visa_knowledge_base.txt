ARTICLE 1: WHAT TO DO IF YOUR VISA CARD IS LOST OR STOLEN
================================================================================
Category: Card Management / Security
Company: Visa
Difficulty: Beginner
Escalation Required: YES (immediate escalation required)

Content:
IMMEDIATE ACTION REQUIRED:

If your Visa card is lost or stolen, act immediately. Every minute counts!

STEP 1: CONTACT YOUR BANK/CARD ISSUER IMMEDIATELY

This is the most important step:
1. Find the phone number on another card, statement, or online banking
2. Call the toll-free number on the back of your card (if you have it)
3. Or contact your bank directly
4. Tell them: "My card is lost/stolen and I need to block it immediately"

Phone Numbers:
- Check your statement for 24/7 support number
- Most banks have dedicated lost/stolen card hotline
- Have your account number ready

What the Bank Will Do:
- Block your card immediately to prevent unauthorized use
- Cancel existing card
- Discuss emergency replacement options
- Review your recent transactions

STEP 2: REPORT TO VISA

While your bank handles the card, also notify Visa:

Online at Visa.com:
1. Visit the "Lost or Stolen Card" page on visa.com
2. Fill out the online form
3. Provide card details and information
4. Submit the report

By Phone:
1. Call Visa's Consumer Services (number varies by country)
2. For USA: 1-800-847-2911 (toll-free)
3. For India: 000-800-100-1219
4. Global numbers available on visa.co.in/support.html

Important Information to Have Ready:
- Full 16-digit card number (if you remember it)
- Expiration date
- Your name
- Date you last used the card
- Date you discovered it missing
- Approximate location where it was lost

STEP 3: REQUEST EMERGENCY REPLACEMENT CARD

If You're Traveling:
- Visa works with your bank to send emergency card
- Usually arrives within 1-3 business days
- Can be sent to your current location
- May require advance payment for expedited shipping
- Call +1-303-967-1096 if outside the USA (reverse charge calls accepted)

Process:
1. Contact your bank immediately
2. Provide current address where you need card shipped
3. Ask about emergency card options
4. May need to provide emergency funds upfront
5. Card will be mailed or available for local pickup

STEP 4: MONITOR YOUR ACCOUNT

After Reporting:
1. Log into online banking daily to check for unauthorized charges
2. Review your statements for next 60 days minimum
3. Set up transaction alerts for unusual activity
4. Visa's Zero Liability Policy protects you from unauthorized charges
5. Report any suspicious transactions immediately

PROTECT YOURSELF GOING FORWARD:

To Prevent Future Loss:
1. Keep card in secure location
2. Don't write PIN on card
3. Avoid carrying multiple cards
4. Use RFID-blocking wallet
5. Enable transaction alerts via mobile app
6. Set spending limits if available

ESCALATION TRIGGERS:

Contact Support Immediately if:
- Lost card already has unauthorized charges
- Card was lost at a crime scene (robbery, mugging)
- Card contains sensitive security features
- You're having difficulty reaching your bank
- You need emergency assistance while traveling

Note: Visa does not process the card replacement directly - only your bank can. Visa provides the framework and expedited support services.

ARTICLE 2: CARD DECLINED - UNDERSTANDING WHY AND HOW TO FIX IT
================================================================================
Category: Card Issues / Troubleshooting
Company: Visa
Difficulty: Beginner
Escalation Required: Sometimes

Content:
WHY WAS MY CARD DECLINED?

Your card can be declined for many reasons. Understanding the cause is the first step to fixing it.

COMMON REASONS FOR CARD DECLINES:

1. EXPIRED CARD
Cause: Your card has passed its expiration date
Solution:
- Check the expiration date on your card (front, usually MM/YY format)
- If expired, contact your bank for replacement
- Use new card number and expiration once you receive it

2. INSUFFICIENT FUNDS
Cause: Your account balance is below the transaction amount
Solution:
- Check your available balance
- For credit cards: Check remaining credit limit
- For debit cards: Verify funds in checking account
- Wait for pending transactions to clear if needed
- Try again if expecting a deposit

3. INCORRECT PIN OR WRONG INFORMATION
Cause: You entered wrong PIN at ATM/terminal
Solution:
- Verify correct PIN (3-4 digits usually)
- Try again, being careful to enter correct numbers
- If locked out after too many attempts, contact bank
- Note: Most ATMs allow 3 attempts before blocking

4. SPENDING/CREDIT LIMIT REACHED
Cause: You've exceeded your daily or credit limit
Solution:
- Check your current available credit or balance
- Wait for your next billing cycle or payment
- Request credit limit increase from issuer
- Contact bank: limits vary by account and time

5. CARD FLAGGED BY BANK'S FRAUD DETECTION
Cause: Unusual activity triggers bank's security system
Reasons:
- Transaction in different country
- Unusually large purchase
- Multiple rapid transactions
- Transaction type unusual for your account
- Geographic inconsistency (card used in two places too quickly)

Solutions:
- Contact your bank to "unlock" the card
- Confirm the transaction was legitimate
- Update bank if traveling internationally
- Answer security questions if required
- Wait 24-48 hours before trying again
- Use card in person if possible (more secure than online)

6. BILLING ADDRESS MISMATCH (Address Verification System)
Cause: Zip code or address on file doesn't match what you entered
Solution:
- Double-check your billing address
- Verify zip code matches exactly what bank has on file
- For debit cards especially: address must match
- Update address in online banking if changed

7. INTERNATIONAL TRANSACTIONS BLOCKED
Cause: Bank blocks transactions in foreign countries by default
Solution:
- Call your bank BEFORE traveling
- Inform them of countries and dates
- Ask them to enable international transactions
- Some banks require 24-48 hours notice
- SMS or call confirmation may be required for first international use

8. 3D SECURE AUTHENTICATION FAILED
Cause: Online security verification didn't complete
What is 3D Secure?
- Additional security layer for online purchases
- Verifies you're legitimate card holder
- Usually requires password or one-time code

Solutions:
- Complete the 3D Secure authentication when prompted
- Check for SMS/email with verification code
- Verify your email address is current with bank
- Some phones may not receive SMS (check network)
- Try again after 15 minutes
- Contact bank if cannot complete verification

9. CARD NOT ACTIVATED YET
Cause: New card hasn't been activated
Solution:
- New cards often require activation
- Look for instructions in card packaging
- Call number on back of card to activate
- Use online banking website to activate
- Wait 24 hours after activation before using

10. MERCHANT RESTRICTIONS
Cause: Merchant won't accept your card type
Why this happens:
- Merchant's payment system doesn't support Visa
- Merchant has minimum purchase amount
- Merchant has maximum amount per transaction
- International merchants may have restrictions
- Certain industries (gambling, etc.) have restrictions

Solutions:
- Ask merchant if they accept Visa
- Check if they have minimum/maximum
- Try a different payment method
- Contact merchant customer service
- Report to Visa if merchant unfairly restricted

TECHNICAL/TEMPORARY DECLINES:

These are usually temporary and resolve on their own:

1. NETWORK/CONNECTIVITY ISSUE
- Merchant's terminal lost connection
- Solution: Wait and retry in 5-10 minutes
- Try a different merchant terminal if available

2. SERVER ERROR
- Payment processor having technical issues
- Solution: Wait and try again later
- Not your fault or card's fault

3. VELOCITY LIMIT (Too Many Transactions Too Fast)
- You made multiple transactions in short timeframe
- System sees as potential fraud
- Solution: Wait 24 hours before trying again

WHAT TO DO WHEN YOUR CARD IS DECLINED:

In Store:
1. Don't panic - usually temporary
2. Ask cashier to try again
3. Try a different terminal if available
4. Offer to provide additional ID for verification
5. Call bank from store if needed
6. Use backup payment method if available

Online:
1. Check entered information is correct
2. Verify billing address and zip code
3. Complete any 3D Secure authentication if prompted
4. Try again in a few minutes
5. Clear browser cookies and try again
6. Try different browser if possible
7. Use payment method directly instead of saved method
8. Contact merchant support if error message appears

At ATM:
1. Most ATMs allow 3 attempts
2. After 3 failures, card is blocked at that ATM for 24 hours
3. Take card and leave (don't try repeatedly)
4. Contact your bank to confirm card is working
5. Try different ATM if needed

WHEN TO CONTACT YOUR BANK:

Contact Your Issuer (Bank) If:
- Card is repeatedly declined for no clear reason
- You see your available balance is incorrect
- Fraud detection seems to be incorrectly triggering
- Card is locked after too many attempts
- You need credit limit increase
- You're receiving error messages you don't understand

What to Tell Them:
1. When your card was declined (date and time)
2. Where it was declined (merchant name, ATM location)
3. Type of transaction (purchase, ATM withdrawal, online)
4. Amount of transaction
5. Error message you received (if any)
6. Whether you've made recent international transactions

Phone Numbers:
- Typically on back of your card
- Most banks: 24/7 customer service
- Have your card number ready

WHEN TO CONTACT VISA:

Visa has limited ability to help directly. Work with your bank first.

Contact Visa if:
- Bank doesn't respond to your issue
- You believe there's a Visa system problem
- You're having issues with 3D Secure specifically
- You want to report a merchant issue
- You need emergency assistance while traveling

Note: Visa does not manage your account - your bank does. Visa's role is to operate the payment network and resolve system-level issues.

ARTICLE 3: HOW TO DISPUTE A TRANSACTION (CHARGEBACK)
================================================================================
Category: Disputes / Fraud
Company: Visa
Difficulty: Intermediate
Escalation Required: YES (for actual dispute filing)

Content:
UNDERSTANDING DISPUTES AND CHARGEBACKS:

A dispute (or chargeback) is a formal claim with your bank that a transaction was unauthorized, fraudulent, or incorrect.

WHEN CAN YOU DISPUTE A TRANSACTION?

Valid Reasons for Dispute:

1. UNAUTHORIZED TRANSACTION
You didn't make this charge. Period.
- Your card was stolen/lost and fraudulently used
- Someone else had your card information
- Someone hacked your account
- Card number was compromised at merchant

Timeline: Can dispute within 60-120 days

2. FRAUDULENT TRANSACTION
You made the transaction, but it was based on false information.
Examples:
- Merchant promised refund but won't provide
- Product/service never delivered after payment
- Received counterfeit goods instead of authentic
- Service was completely different from advertised
- Duplicate charge (charged twice for one purchase)

Timeline: Usually 60-120 days from transaction

3. MERCHANT ERROR
Transaction was processed incorrectly.
Examples:
- Charged wrong amount (too much money taken)
- Charged multiple times for single transaction
- Card information entered incorrectly
- Amount authorized doesn't match what you used

Timeline: 30-120 days typically

4. NO AUTHORIZATION
Merchant processed transaction without your consent.
Examples:
- Recurring charge after you canceled subscription
- Merchant didn't obtain proper authorization
- Transaction processed after you declined it

Timeline: Varies by specific situation

5. NO DELIVERY
You paid for goods/services that never arrived.
Examples:
- Package was supposed to arrive but didn't
- Service wasn't provided despite payment
- Partial delivery (didn't receive everything)

Timeline: Usually 30-60 days after expected delivery

WHEN YOU CANNOT DISPUTE (Visa Won't Allow):

Disputes NOT Allowed:
- You change your mind about purchase (buyer's remorse)
- Merchandise quality is poor (but not counterfeit)
- Airline seat selection wasn't what you wanted
- You made transaction by mistake
- Merchant delivered product but you're unhappy
- You gave someone else your card permission to use it

STEP-BY-STEP DISPUTE PROCESS:

STEP 1: GATHER EVIDENCE FIRST (Before Contacting Bank)

Essential Documents:
- Order confirmation emails
- Shipping/tracking information
- Delivery proof (or lack thereof)
- Communication with merchant (emails, chat logs)
- Credit card statement showing transaction
- Proof of payment refusal (if applicable)
- Photos of damaged goods (if applicable)
- Your attempts to contact merchant

For Unauthorized Charges:
- Bank statement showing charge
- Proof you reported card lost/stolen
- Confirmation card was blocked
- Your location at time of transaction

STEP 2: TRY TO RESOLVE WITH MERCHANT FIRST

Before Disputing:
1. Contact merchant customer service
2. Clearly explain the problem
3. Request refund in writing (email preferred)
4. Allow 5-7 business days for response
5. Escalate to manager/supervisor if needed
6. Document all communication

Why Try First?
- Faster resolution if successful
- Avoids chargeback fees
- Preserves merchant relationship
- Gives merchant chance to make right

STEP 3: CONTACT YOUR BANK

If merchant won't help:

1. Call your bank (number on back of card)
2. Say: "I want to dispute a transaction"
3. Bank will open a dispute/chargeback case
4. Provide transaction details:
   - Merchant name
   - Transaction date
   - Amount
   - Your transaction ID (if available)
   - Reason for dispute

5. Submit required documentation:
   - Signed dispute form (bank will send)
   - Copies of all evidence
   - Written statement explaining issue
   - Supporting documents

STEP 4: BANK INVESTIGATES

What Happens Next:
1. Bank acknowledges receipt of dispute
2. Bank assigns investigation timeframe (30-60 days typically)
3. Bank requests information from merchant
4. Merchant has time to respond with evidence
5. Both sides present their case
6. Bank makes determination

During Investigation:
- You can provide additional evidence
- Bank may ask follow-up questions
- Merchant may contact you directly
- Process is confidential

STEP 5: RESOLUTION

Possible Outcomes:

Case Approved (In Your Favor):
- Chargeback issued
- Funds returned to your account
- Takes 5-7 days after approval
- Merchant's account is debited
- Merchant may be penalized

Case Denied (Not In Your Favor):
- Dispute rejected
- Chargeback not issued
- Funds not returned
- You can appeal (request reconsideration)
- Have 5-7 days to provide new evidence

TIMELINE:

Typical Dispute Process:
- Initial report: Immediate
- Provisional credit: 5-10 days (if applicable)
- Investigation: 30-60 days
- Final decision: 10 days after investigation ends
- Chargeback posted: 5-7 days if approved

Total: 45-90 days typically

PROVISIONAL CREDIT:

What Is It?
- Temporary credit while bank investigates
- Not final - can be reversed if case is lost
- Gives you access to funds immediately
- Appears as separate line on statement

Will I Get Provisional Credit?
- Usually yes for unauthorized fraud
- Maybe for other dispute types
- Not guaranteed
- Depends on bank's policy

AFTER WINNING A DISPUTE:

If dispute approved:
1. Funds returned to account (takes 5-7 days)
2. Merchant receives chargeback notice
3. Merchant may contact you (legally can)
4. Merchant may dispute your dispute (second-level process)
5. Merchant may refuse to do business with you

After Dispute is Final:
- Funds stay with you (unless merchant wins appeal)
- Merchant bears cost of chargeback
- Can proceed with legal action against merchant if needed

SPECIAL CASES:

RECURRING CHARGES (Subscription/Membership):

If Merchant Won't Cancel:
1. Contact merchant first to cancel
2. Request written confirmation of cancellation
3. Verify charge no longer appears on next billing cycle
4. If still charges despite cancellation request, dispute

Dispute Reason: "Subscription not cancelled as requested"

Timeline: Dispute within 60 days of unauthorized recurring charge

CHARGEBACKS vs REFUNDS:

Refund:
- Request directly from merchant
- Funds returned within 3-5 business days
- No investigation needed
- Preferred method

Chargeback:
- Filed through your bank
- Takes 45-90 days
- Investigation required
- Merchant may be penalized
- Last resort after merchant refuses refund

Try Refund First, Chargeback As Last Resort.

DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST:

Before Submitting Dispute, Have:
- [ ] Original transaction receipt
- [ ] Bank statement showing charge
- [ ] Order confirmation email
- [ ] Email communications with merchant
- [ ] Tracking/shipping information
- [ ] Photos of goods (if applicable)
- [ ] Proof of delivery issues
- [ ] Written proof of merchant refusal
- [ ] Request for refund in writing
- [ ] Your statement of facts (1-2 pages)

Complete documentation = stronger case = better outcome

ARTICLE 4: FRAUD PROTECTION AND UNAUTHORIZED TRANSACTIONS
================================================================================
Category: Security / Fraud
Company: Visa
Difficulty: Beginner
Escalation Required: YES (if fraud detected)

Content:
UNDERSTANDING VISA FRAUD PROTECTION:

Visa has multiple layers of protection to prevent fraud, and protects you if fraud occurs.

VISA'S FRAUD DETECTION SYSTEMS:

Advanced Authorization (AI-Powered):
- Visa's AI analyzes every transaction in real-time
- Checks 500+ data points per transaction
- Learns your typical spending patterns
- Identifies suspicious activity before charging
- Helps prevent $25+ billion in fraud annually

What It Checks:
- Transaction amount (is it typical for you?)
- Location (is it possible to be there?)
- Merchant type (do you normally shop here?)
- Time of day (unusual time for you?)
- Device information
- IP address
- Velocity (too many transactions too fast?)
- Biometrics (if available)

How It Works:
- Legitimate transactions usually approved instantly
- Suspicious transactions may require verification
- You might get text asking to confirm transaction
- False declines minimized while catching fraud

3D SECURE AUTHENTICATION:

What Is It?
- Additional security layer for online shopping
- Verifies you're the legitimate card holder
- Happens between you, issuer bank, and merchant
- Protects both you and merchant from fraud

How It Works:
1. You enter card details at online merchant
2. Merchant checks if 3D Secure is available
3. You're redirected to your bank's authentication page
4. You verify via password, PIN, or biometric
5. Authentication confirmed back to merchant
6. Transaction proceeds (if authentication successful)

You Don't Need to Register:
- 3D Secure is automatic
- Your bank manages it
- You just authenticate when prompted
- No separate signup needed

Note: Visa does not set up the authentication - your bank does.

ZERO LIABILITY PROTECTION:

What Is Visa's Zero Liability Policy?

Visa Zero Liability means:
- You're not responsible for unauthorized charges
- Even if card/number was fraudulently used
- Protection covers most Visa transactions
- Applies to credit and debit cards

Exceptions (Visa Doesn't Cover):
- Certain commercial card transactions
- Anonymous prepaid card transactions
- Transactions made by someone you gave permission to
- Gross negligence (you wrote PIN on card, for example)
- Cases of fraud on your part

What It Covers:
- Lost card used without permission
- Stolen card information
- Online fraud with your card details
- ATM fraud
- Phone/mail order fraud
- Counterfeit card use

Important Requirement:
You MUST report unauthorized transactions promptly:
- Contact bank within 60 days
- The sooner you report, the better
- Visa's Zero Liability requires timely notification
- Delaying reporting may void protection

WHAT TO DO IF YOU SEE UNAUTHORIZED CHARGES:

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (Within Hours):

1. Contact Your Bank Immediately
   - Call 24/7 number on back of card
   - Or log into online banking to report
   - Tell them: "I see unauthorized charges"
   - Provide transaction details

2. Your Bank Will:
   - Freeze/cancel card immediately
   - Block merchant from further charges
   - Reverse unauthorized charges (temporarily)
   - May send replacement card
   - May provide emergency cash

3. Document Everything:
   - Screenshot unauthorized charges
   - Note exact amounts and dates
   - Record merchant names
   - Save all communications

WITHIN 24-48 HOURS:

4. File Formal Dispute
   - Submit written dispute form
   - Include documentation
   - Provide your account number
   - Explain why charges are unauthorized

5. Change Any Compromised Passwords
   - If online account was hacked
   - Change bank password
   - Change email password (if that was compromised)
   - Change passwords for other accounts with same email

6. Monitor Your Account
   - Check daily for 60 days
   - Set up transaction alerts
   - Watch for new unauthorized charges
   - Watch for identity theft signs

FRAUD INVESTIGATION PROCESS:

After You Report:
1. Bank opens fraud investigation
2. Investigates charges (15-30 days typically)
3. Issues provisional credit (usually 5-10 days)
4. Communicates with merchant's bank
5. Merchant disputes (if they believe charges were legit)
6. Further investigation if merchant disputes
7. Final determination (45-60 days from reporting)

Timeline:
- Provisional credit: 5-10 days
- Investigation: 30-60 days
- Final decision: Additional 10-20 days if disputed

During Investigation:
- You have access to provisional credit
- Can use funds while investigation proceeds
- If investigation goes against you, funds can be reversed
- Bank must be able to prove charges were authorized

WHAT MAKES A TRANSACTION LOOK FRAUDULENT:

Your Bank Might Decline or Flag:

1. UNUSUAL LOCATION
- Card used in different country
- Card used in state you rarely visit
- Geographic impossibility (two cities too far apart, too fast)

2. UNUSUAL MERCHANT TYPE
- Different type of purchase than you normally make
- High-risk merchant (cash advance, gambling, etc.)
- New merchant you've never used

3. UNUSUAL AMOUNT
- Amount much higher than your typical purchase
- Multiple large purchases in short timeframe
- Round numbers that seem suspicious

4. UNUSUAL TIMING
- Transaction at unusual hour (3 AM, for example)
- Rapid succession of transactions
- Pattern suggesting testing stolen card

5. UNUSUAL DEVICE/LOCATION
- Different device than normal
- Different IP address
- VPN detected
- Transaction from country you're not in

What Happens:
- May decline automatically (security)
- May call/text you to confirm
- May require additional authentication
- May temporarily block card
- All to prevent fraud

PROTECTING YOURSELF FROM FRAUD:

Best Practices:

Online Shopping:
- Use strong, unique passwords for each site
- Use credit card over debit (more fraud protection)
- Don't save payment details on untrusted sites
- Look for "https://" and lock icon
- Verify site is legitimate (check URL carefully)
- Don't shop on public WiFi without VPN
- Ignore phishing emails asking for card details

In Person:
- Keep card in sight when making payment
- Don't let server take card out of sight
- Cover keypad when entering PIN
- Keep receipts and compare to statements
- Sign back of card
- Don't write PIN on card or nearby

Card Information:
- Don't share full card number unless necessary
- Don't use card number in emails
- Don't text card information
- Don't read number aloud in public
- Be wary of requests for CVV (3 digits on back)

Statements and Monitoring:
- Review statements monthly
- Check online banking regularly
- Set up transaction alerts
- Sign up for free credit monitoring
- Consider credit freeze if high risk
- Monitor credit report annually

Traveling:
- Notify bank before traveling
- Use ATMs in secure locations only
- Avoid currency exchange in unfamiliar areas
- Keep card separate from wallet/ID
- Avoid leaving card unattended

PHISHING AND SCAMS:

Be Suspicious Of:
- Emails from "Visa" asking you to confirm account
- Unexpected calls asking for card details
- Texts claiming card is locked
- Pop-ups asking for payment information
- Offers that seem too good to be true

What to Do:
- DO NOT click links in suspicious emails
- DO NOT provide card details via email/phone/text
- Contact Visa or your bank directly instead
- Hang up phone calls and call back official number
- Forward phishing emails to your bank
- Report scams to Federal Trade Commission (FTC)

Remember: Legitimate companies never ask for card details via email, phone, or unsolicited messages.

ARTICLE 5: CARD FEATURES AND TECHNICAL ISSUES
================================================================================
Category: Card Features / Troubleshooting
Company: Visa
Difficulty: Beginner
Escalation Required: Sometimes

Content:
VISA CARD TYPES:

Credit Cards:
- You borrow money from the card issuer
- You pay back the amount monthly
- Charged interest on unpaid balance
- Build credit history
- Best fraud protection

Debit Cards:
- Money is taken directly from your bank account
- No interest charged
- No credit building
- Still protected by Visa's fraud prevention
- Funds taken immediately

Prepaid Cards:
- You load money onto the card upfront
- Spend until balance is depleted
- No credit building
- Higher fees typically
- Good for budgeting or gifts

Visa Gift Cards:
- Pre-loaded with specific amount
- Given as gift
- Can be used anywhere Visa is accepted
- Expires after certain date
- No refunds once spent

ATM/Debit Card Features:
- Can withdraw cash from ATMs
- Can make deposits (if issuer supports)
- Can check balance at ATM
- 24/7 availability

FINDING AN ATM:

Visa ATM Locator:
1. Visit visa.com/atmlocator
2. Enter your location or address
3. Shows nearby ATMs
4. Can filter by bank/network
5. Over 2 million ATMs worldwide

Using Visa ATMs:
- Can withdraw cash at any Visa-enabled ATM
- Check your bank for fee information
- Different ATM networks may charge fees
- Your bank may reimburse out-of-network fees
- PIN required (usually 4 digits)

ATM Cash Withdrawal Issues:

"ATM Out of Service"
- ATM temporarily down
- Try different ATM
- Contact your bank if you need cash urgently

"Withdrawal Limit Exceeded"
- Daily withdrawal limit reached
- Limits vary by bank (typically $200-$500)
- Contact bank to increase limit
- Can withdraw next day

"Incorrect PIN"
- Wrong PIN entered
- Most ATMs allow 3 attempts
- After 3 failures, card blocked at that ATM for 24 hours
- Try different ATM
- Don't try repeatedly at same machine

"ATM Ate Your Card"
- Machine kept your card
- Usually for security (too many failed PINs)
- Card will be mailed back by the ATM operator
- Contact your bank for replacement
- Takes 5-7 business days to return

TRAVELING WITH YOUR VISA CARD:

Before You Travel:
1. Notify your bank of travel dates and countries
2. Ask about foreign transaction fees
3. Ask about currency conversion rates
4. Request emergency card shipping if needed
5. Get list of ATMs in destination country
6. Make note of 24/7 support number

Informed Traveler Checklist:
- [ ] Bank knows travel dates
- [ ] Card not set to block international transactions
- [ ] Backup payment method (different card)
- [ ] Copies of important numbers (separate from card)
- [ ] Contact info for your bank
- [ ] Travel insurance (if applicable)
- [ ] Mobile phone working internationally

Traveling Safely:
1. Use ATMs in secure, well-lit locations
2. Prefer bank ATMs over standalone machines
3. Cover keypad when entering PIN
4. Don't accept help from strangers
5. Keep card separate from wallet
6. Avoid carrying large amounts of cash
7. Use card in reputable establishments
8. Report lost card immediately

Currency and Exchange:
- Visa operates in most currencies
- Dynamic currency conversion at merchant level
- Exchange rates vary by issuer and merchant
- Check with your bank about rates
- ATM withdrawals typically have better rates

MERCHANT RESTRICTIONS:

What Are Merchant Restrictions?

Some merchants have rules about Visa acceptance:

Minimum Amounts:
- Some require minimum purchase (e.g., $5 minimum)
- Legal in some countries with restrictions
- Contact merchant if refused for being below minimum

Maximum Amounts:
- Some set maximum per transaction
- Usually for security reasons
- Contact merchant if need to split transaction

Industry Restrictions:
- Some merchants have special requirements
- Gambling, adult sites may have additional verification
- Some international merchants have restrictions
- Government agencies may have specific rules

Merchant Refusal:
- Merchant must accept all Visa cards equally
- Cannot refuse based on card type (debit vs credit)
- Cannot refuse based on issuer
- Can report discriminatory behavior to Visa

If You Believe Merchant Refused Unjustly:
1. Ask for manager
2. Explain you have valid Visa card
3. Request they process transaction
4. Document merchant name, location, time
5. If still refused, report to Visa via online form

DAMAGED OR COMPROMISED CARDS:

Damaged Card:
- Unreadable magnetic stripe
- Chip not working
- Card warped or bent
- Torn or physically damaged

Solution:
1. Contact your bank
2. Request replacement card
3. Bank will send new card (5-7 business days)
4. Don't try to use damaged card (may fail)
5. Destroy old card by cutting in half

Compromised Card (Number Stolen):
- Someone has your card number
- Card itself wasn't physically lost
- May not know how it was compromised
- Security concern even if card in hand

Solution:
1. Contact bank immediately
2. Report suspected fraud
3. Request card replacement as precaution
4. Request cardless/temporary card if available
5. Monitor account carefully

Lost During Travel:
- In foreign country and lost card
- Need emergency card
- Cannot wait for regular mailing

UNDERSTANDING VISA'S ROLE:

Important Note:
Visa does NOT manage your account - your bank/card issuer does.

Visa operates the payment network. Your bank:
- Issues the card
- Manages your account
- Handles disputes
- Processes payments
- Manages your balance
- Handles fraud investigations
- Controls limits and settings

Therefore:
- Contact your bank FIRST for account issues
- Contact Visa for network-level issues
- Only escalate to Visa if bank won't help

WHEN TO CONTACT YOUR BANK (Not Visa):

Account Issues (Contact Your Bank):
- Can't log into online banking
- Need to report lost/stolen card (though Visa can help too)
- Card declined and need to understand why
- Want to increase credit limit
- Billing questions
- Statement discrepancies
- Transaction questions
- Change account settings
- Request replacement card

Fraudulent Charges (Contact Your Bank):
- Unauthorized transactions on your account
- Dispute/chargeback process
- Fraud investigation
- Provisional credit

Card-Specific Issues (Contact Your Bank):
- PIN problems
- Card activation
- Card replacement
- Card features
- Account limits

WHEN TO CONTACT VISA:

Network-Level Issues (Contact Visa):
- Visa network appears to be down
- 3D Secure authentication not working
- Card accepted by merchant but later declined
- Merchant claiming card was invalid when it wasn't
- Visa network fraud detection incorrectly blocking legitimate transactions
- Merchant disputing your chargeback unfairly
- Security issue with Visa network
- Reporting merchant for discriminatory practices
- Reporting suspicious communication claiming to be from Visa
- General questions about Visa products

Escalation From Bank (Contact Visa):
- Your bank refused to investigate your dispute
- Your bank not honoring Zero Liability protection
- Your bank refusing to address fraud
- Bank mistreated you during dispute process
- Feel Visa needs to directly intervene
