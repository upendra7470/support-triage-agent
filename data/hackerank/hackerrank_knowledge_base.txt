ARTICLE 1: HOW TO LOG IN TO YOUR HACKERRANK ACCOUNT
================================================================================
Category: Account Access / Login
Company: HackerRank
Difficulty: Beginner
Escalation Required: No

Content:
To access your HackerRank account, follow these steps:

1. Visit the HackerRank login page at https://www.hackerrank.com/login.html
2. Enter your email address or username associated with your account
3. Enter your password
4. Click "Log In"

You will be directed to your personal dashboard after successful login.

If you're having trouble finding the login page:
- Visit www.hackerrank.com
- Click the "Log In" button in the top-right corner
- You will be taken to the login page

Note: HackerRank has separate login portals for different products:
- HackerRank Community (for coding challenges and practice)
- HackerRank for Work (for hiring and recruitment)
- Make sure you're using the correct portal for your account type

If your login is not working, see related articles:
- "Update or Reset Password"
- "Account Access Troubleshooting"

ARTICLE 2: UPDATE OR RESET PASSWORD
================================================================================
Category: Account Access / Security
Company: HackerRank
Difficulty: Beginner
Escalation Required: No

Content:
CHANGING YOUR PASSWORD (If You Know Your Current Password):

1. Log in to HackerRank using your credentials
2. Click your profile icon in the upper-right corner
3. Select "Settings" from the dropdown menu
4. Locate the "Change Password" section
5. Enter your current password
6. Enter your new password
7. Retype your new password to confirm
8. Click "Save"

Password Requirements:
- Minimum 6 characters long
- Use a combination of letters, numbers, and special characters for added security
- Passwords are case-sensitive

RESETTING YOUR PASSWORD (If You Forgot It):

If you cannot remember your current password:

1. Go to the HackerRank login page (https://www.hackerrank.com/login.html)
2. Click "Forgot Password?" link below the password field
3. Enter the email address associated with your account
4. Select the verification checkbox (if applicable)
5. Click "Send Reset Link" or "Next" depending on your portal
6. Check your email inbox for a password reset link from HackerRank
7. Click the link in the email
8. Follow the instructions to create a new password
9. Log in with your new password

Important Notes:
- Password reset links expire after a certain period (typically 24 hours)
- If you don't see the email, check your spam/junk folder
- Make sure you're using the email address associated with your account
- If you have multiple accounts, you may be resetting the wrong one

TROUBLESHOOTING PASSWORD ISSUES:

Common Problem: "Invalid Email or Username"
Solution: Double-check that you've entered the correct email address associated with your account. Try using your email instead of username or vice versa.

Common Problem: "Password Reset Link Expired"
Solution: Click "Forgot Password?" again and request a new reset link.

Common Problem: "I Don't Receive the Reset Email"
Solution:
1. Check your spam/junk folder
2. Verify the email is correct
3. Wait a few minutes and try again
4. Contact HackerRank support if the issue persists

SECURITY BEST PRACTICES:

- Change your password regularly (at least every 90 days)
- Use unique passwords for HackerRank and other accounts
- Never share your password with anyone
- Be cautious of phishing emails asking for password resets

If you continue to have password reset issues, this may require escalation to HackerRank support for account verification purposes.

Category: Account Settings
Company: HackerRank

Content:
ACCESSING YOUR ACCOUNT SETTINGS:

1. Log in to your HackerRank account

SECTIONS AVAILABLE IN ACCOUNT SETTINGS:

General Profile Information:

- Update your email address
- Change your preferred language

Password Management:
- Change your password (see Article: "Update or Reset Password")

Account Preferences:
- Email notification settings
- Privacy settings (profile visibility, search indexing)
- Language and timezone preferences

Social Connections:
- Connect or disconnect from Google, Facebook, GitHub

Account Type and Tier:

SECURITY RECOMMENDATIONS:

1. Regularly review your connected applications
2. Remove social connections you no longer use
3. Enable Two-Factor Authentication if available
4. Keep your email address up to date
5. Review login activity and active sessions
6. Update privacy settings according to your preferences

ARTICLE 4: BILLING, SUBSCRIPTIONS, AND PAYMENT ISSUES
================================================================================
Category: Billing / Subscriptions
Company: HackerRank
Difficulty: Intermediate
Escalation Required: Sometimes (if payment issues persist)

Content:
UNDERSTANDING HACKERRANK SUBSCRIPTION TIERS:

HackerRank offers multiple subscription options:

Free Tier:
- Access to basic coding challenges
- Limited practice problems
- Community features
- No cost

Pro/Premium Tier:
- Unlimited access to all coding challenges
- Advanced problem sets and contests
- Interview preparation resources
- Monthly or annual payment required

For Work/Hiring Plans:
- Custom technical assessments for hiring
- Advanced recruiting features
- Admin and team management
- Contact sales for pricing

VIEWING YOUR CURRENT SUBSCRIPTION:

1. Log in to your HackerRank account
2. Click your profile icon → Settings
3. Look for "Subscription" or "Billing" section
4. View your current tier, renewal date, and payment method

UPGRADING YOUR SUBSCRIPTION:

1. In your account settings, find the subscription section
2. Click "Upgrade" or "Upgrade Now"
3. Select your preferred plan (monthly or annual recommended for discount)
4. Enter payment information
5. Complete the purchase
6. Your upgrade is effective immediately

PAYMENT METHOD MANAGEMENT:

1. Go to Settings → Billing or Subscription section
2. Click "Update Payment Method" or "Manage Billing"
3. Add or update your credit/debit card
4. Remove old or expired payment methods
5. Save your changes

BILLING ISSUES AND TROUBLESHOOTING:

Problem: "Payment Declined"
Possible Causes:
- Insufficient funds in your account
- Card expired or blocked
- Incorrect billing address or zip code
- Card issuer declined the transaction for security reasons
- 3D Secure authentication required

Solutions:
1. Verify your card details are correct
2. Contact your bank to ensure the card is active
3. Try a different payment method
4. Wait 24 hours and retry if a temporary block occurred
5. Ensure your billing address matches your bank's records
6. If issue persists, contact HackerRank support with order details

Problem: "Duplicate Charges or Unexpected Billings"
Solutions:
1. Check your email for confirmation receipts
2. Review your billing history in account settings
3. Verify the renewal date of your subscription
4. If charged multiple times, contact HackerRank support immediately with:
   - Transaction ID or Order number
   - Dates of suspicious charges
   - Screenshots of your billing statement

Problem: "Subscription Not Active After Purchase"
Solutions:
1. Refresh your browser or log out and back in
2. Allow 5-10 minutes for the system to process
3. Check your email for confirmation
4. Verify the payment method was successfully charged
5. Contact support if still not activated after 24 hours

REFUNDS AND CANCELLATION:

HackerRank Refund Policy:
- Refunds are typically issued within 7-30 days of cancellation
- Early refunds may be available depending on your plan terms
- Contact support for refund requests

How to Cancel Your Subscription:
1. Go to Settings → Subscription
2. Click "Cancel Subscription" or "Manage Subscription"
3. Confirm cancellation (you may be offered a retention discount)
4. Your access continues until the end of your billing period
5. After the billing period, your account reverts to free tier

Important Note: Canceling does not delete your account data; it simply downgrades your access level.

BILLING ESCALATION TRIGGERS:

Contact HackerRank support if:
- Multiple payment attempts have failed
- You see unauthorized charges
- You were double-charged
- Your refund hasn't been processed after 30 days
- You need a custom billing arrangement
- You're experiencing recurring billing issues

ARTICLE 5: ACCOUNT SECURITY AND UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS
================================================================================
Category: Security / Account Compromise
Company: HackerRank
Difficulty: Intermediate
Escalation Required: Yes (if account is compromised)

Content:
RECOGNIZING UNAUTHORIZED ACCOUNT ACCESS:

Warning Signs:
- You see login activity you don't recognize
- Your password changed without your action
- Your email address was changed
- Unexpected progress or problem submissions in your account
- You receive notifications for actions you didn't perform
- Your profile information has been altered
- You can't log in with your usual password

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS IF YOUR ACCOUNT IS COMPROMISED:

STEP 1: Secure Your Password
1. If you can still log in, immediately change your password
   - Go to Settings → Change Password
   - Use a strong, unique password (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, special characters)
2. If you cannot log in:
   - Go to the forgot password page
   - Reset your password using your email
   - Make sure the email is correct

STEP 2: Secure Your Email Address
1. Log in to the email account associated with your HackerRank account
2. Change that email account's password as well
3. Review email forwarding rules and recovery options
4. Check for suspicious account recovery attempts

STEP 3: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (If Available)
1. Go to Settings → Security or Two-Factor Authentication
2. Follow the setup process
3. Use an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.)
4. Save backup codes in a secure location

STEP 4: Review Account Activity
1. In Settings, look for "Login Activity" or "Active Sessions"
2. Review all recent logins
3. Log out suspicious sessions
4. Note any unfamiliar devices or locations

STEP 5: ESCALATE TO HACKERRANK SUPPORT
Contact HackerRank support immediately and provide:
- Your account email
- When you first noticed the compromise
- Description of what was changed or accessed
- Any relevant screenshots
- Your current location and typical login locations

This requires human verification and escalation to support team.

PREVENTING FUTURE COMPROMISE:

Best Practices:
1. Use strong, unique passwords for HackerRank and other accounts
2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication on all important accounts
3. Don't share your password with anyone (HackerRank staff will never ask)
4. Be cautious of phishing emails claiming to be from HackerRank
5. Keep your device secure with updated software and antivirus
6. Use public WiFi carefully; consider a VPN
7. Log out from shared computers
8. Regularly review your account activity

RECOGNIZING PHISHING ATTEMPTS:

Be suspicious if:
- You receive emails asking to "verify your account" or "confirm password"
- Links in emails don't lead to official HackerRank domains
- Sender email looks slightly off (e.g., hackerank.com instead of hackerrank.com)
- You're asked for payment details via email
- The email has poor grammar or formatting

What to Do:
- Do NOT click any links in suspicious emails
- Do NOT provide your password or personal information
- Forward the email to HackerRank support
- Delete the email

REPORTING SECURITY ISSUES:

If you discover a security vulnerability or unauthorized account access:
1. Do not attempt to "test" the vulnerability further
2. Contact HackerRank support immediately
3. Provide detailed information about the issue
4. For critical security flaws, visit the security page on HackerRank.com

ARTICLE 6: COMMON HACKERRANK ERRORS AND TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================
Category: Technical Support / Troubleshooting
Company: HackerRank
Difficulty: Intermediate
Escalation Required: Sometimes

Content:
COMMON ERROR MESSAGES AND SOLUTIONS:

Error: "Sorry, we can't find your account"
Context: Appears when trying to log in
Causes:
- Wrong email/username entered
- Account doesn't exist with that email
- Typo in email address
- Mixing up HackerRank Community vs HackerRank for Work accounts

Solutions:
1. Double-check your email spelling
2. Try using your username instead of email (or vice versa)
3. Try the forgot password flow to verify the account exists
4. If you have multiple accounts, try the other portal
5. If still not found, escalate to support (may need to create new account)

Error: "Invalid Credentials"
Context: Appears after entering username/password
Causes:
- Incorrect password
- Account doesn't exist
- Login attempt from blocked IP or suspicious location

Solutions:
1. Verify caps lock is off
2. Re-enter your credentials carefully
3. Click "Forgot Password?" to reset your password
4. Wait 5 minutes before retrying if you see this repeatedly
5. Try from a different device or network if possible

Error: "Execution Environment Error" or "Code Submission Failed"
Context: When attempting to submit code solutions
Causes:
- Server-side issue with the coding environment
- Network connectivity problem
- Browser compatibility issue
- Session timeout

Solutions:
1. Refresh the page and try again
2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
3. Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
4. Check your internet connection
5. Wait a few minutes and retry if servers are experiencing issues
6. Contact support if error persists

Error: "Access Denied" or "Permission Denied"
Context: When trying to access certain features or problems
Causes:
- You don't have a required subscription level
- The problem is restricted to certain regions
- Your account type doesn't have access
- Content is temporarily unavailable

Solutions:
1. Verify your subscription level in Settings
2. If free user, consider upgrading to Pro
3. Check if the content is region-restricted
4. Ensure you're logged into the correct account

Error: "Session Expired"
Context: After inactivity or when revisiting the site
Causes:
- Your login session timed out (usually after 2-4 hours of inactivity)
- Cookie settings on your browser
- Network interruption

Solutions:
1. Log in again
2. Check browser cookie settings are enabled
3. Make sure you're not in private/incognito mode (if possible)
4. Try a different browser

PLATFORM AVAILABILITY AND MAINTENANCE:

HackerRank regularly performs maintenance. During maintenance windows:
- Features may be temporarily unavailable
- Code submissions may fail
- Performance may be slow
- Leaderboards may not update

What to Do:
- Check the HackerRank status page or announcements
- Wait for maintenance to complete
- Try again after the maintenance window
- No action needed; you're not blocked

BROWSER AND COMPATIBILITY ISSUES:

Recommended Browsers:
- Chrome (latest version)
- Firefox (latest version)
- Safari (latest version)
- Edge (latest version)

Troubleshooting Browser Issues:
1. Update your browser to the latest version
2. Disable browser extensions (especially ad blockers and script blockers)
3. Clear browser cache and cookies
4. Disable "Do Not Track" setting if enabled
5. Try a different browser to test if it's browser-specific
6. Ensure JavaScript is enabled

NETWORK AND CONNECTIVITY:

If experiencing slow performance or timeouts:
1. Check your internet speed (speedtest.net)
2. Close other bandwidth-heavy applications
3. Restart your router/modem
4. Move closer to your WiFi router
5. Try a wired connection if possible
6. Try at a different time (may be server congestion)

ARTICLE 7: HIRING FEATURES AND HACKERRANK FOR WORK
================================================================================
Category: For Work / Hiring
Company: HackerRank
Difficulty: Intermediate
Escalation Required: Sometimes

Content:
HACKERRANK FOR WORK vs HACKERRANK COMMUNITY:

HackerRank for Work is a separate platform designed for:
- Companies to create custom technical assessments
- Recruiting teams to evaluate candidates
- Organizations to manage hiring workflows

HackerRank Community is for:
- Individual developers to practice coding
- Users to participate in contests
- Learning and skill development

IMPORTANT: These are separate accounts. You may have both.

IMPERSONATION DETECTION:

HackerRank for Work includes sophisticated impersonation detection:
- AI-based behavior analysis during coding assessments
- Detection of suspicious patterns indicating someone else may be completing the assessment
- Flagging of potential dishonest behavior for review

If You're Taking an Assessment:
- Complete the assessment yourself
- Don't have someone else help
- Avoid external tools for completing code unless explicitly allowed
- Be aware that unusual patterns may be flagged for review

If You're Running Assessments (Hiring):
- You'll see detailed reports on candidate behavior
- Impersonation flags appear in results
- You can review video recording of assessment sessions (if enabled)
- Use this data as one factor in evaluation

SUBMITTING SUPPORT REQUESTS:

To contact HackerRank support:
1. Go to https://portal.usepylon.com/hackerrank-support/forms/customer-request-form
2. Fill in the form with:
   - Your company name
   - Your name and email
   - Problem category
   - Detailed description of your issue
   - Screenshots if applicable
3. Submit the form
4. You'll receive a response from support

ESCALATION REQUIRED FOR:
- Account access issues
- Payment problems
- Custom assessment help
- Technical assessment failures
- Reporting security issues
- Custom hiring workflows

ARTICLE 8: WHEN TO CONTACT HACKERRANK SUPPORT
================================================================================
Category: Support / Escalation
Company: HackerRank
Difficulty: Beginner
Escalation Required: Yes (for actual support request)

Content:
SELF-HELP OPTIONS (Try These First):

Before contacting support, try:
1. Check the HackerRank Knowledge Base at https://support.hackerrank.com/
2. Search for your issue in the knowledge base
3. Check your email for HackerRank notifications or status updates
4. Review your browser console for error messages (press F12)
5. Try logging out completely and logging back in
6. Clear your browser cache and try again
7. Try a different browser or device

When you contact support, you'll need to provide:
- Your account email
- Detailed description of the issue
- Steps you've already taken to resolve it
- Error messages (exact text or screenshots)
- Your device and browser information
- When the issue started

ISSUES THAT SHOULD BE ESCALATED:

Account & Access:
- Cannot log in despite resetting password
- Account appears to be compromised or hacked
- Email address associated with account was hacked
- Cannot receive password reset emails
- Account locked or suspended

Billing Issues:
- Unauthorized charges or duplicate billing
- Payment declined repeatedly
- Refund not received within 30 days
- Need to modify subscription arrangement

Technical Issues:
- Widespread feature failures
- Code submission consistently failing
- Assessment platform completely down
- Performance severely impacting usage

Security Issues:
- Suspected data breach
- Unauthorized access to account
- Phishing or scam attempts
- API security concerns (for For Work)

How to Submit a Support Request:
1. Visit https://support.hackerrank.com/
2. Click "Submit a Request" button
3. Choose your category carefully
4. Provide all relevant details
5. Include any error messages or screenshots
6. Submit the ticket
7. You'll receive a ticket number and tracking information

Support Response Time:
- Urgent issues: 4-24 hours
- Standard issues: 24-48 hours
- General questions: 2-5 business days
- Response time varies based on issue complexity
